Tourist Attractions

Mysuru

Mysuru is popularly known as the City of Palaces. It is famous for its royal heritage, beautiful architecture and the grand celebration of Mysuru Dasara. Mysore Palace is its most famous attraction.

Coorg

Coorg is a beautiful hill destination known for its coffee plantations, misty mountains, green forests and pleasant weather. It is also famous for its natural beauty and peaceful atmosphere.

Hampi

Hampi is a UNESCO World Heritage Site famous for its ancient temples, stone monuments and magnificent ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire.

Gokarna

Gokarna is a coastal town known for its beautiful beaches, scenic views and peaceful surroundings. Om Beach and Kudle Beach are among its popular attractions.

Bengaluru

Bengaluru is the capital of Karnataka and a major centre for technology, education and business. Popular attractions include Lalbagh Botanical Garden, Cubbon Park, Bengaluru Palace and Vidhana Soudha.
