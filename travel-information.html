Travel Information

Best Time to Visit Karnataka

  • October to February: Pleasant weather for sightseeing, exploring historical places and visiting hill stations.
  • March to May: Suitable for exploring certain destinations, although some regions can be quite hot.
  • June to September: Monsoon season, ideal for enjoying waterfalls, greenery and scenic landscapes, but heavy rainfall may affect travel in some areas.

Travel Tips

  • Carry comfortable clothing and footwear.
  • Check weather conditions before travelling.
  • Book accommodation and transportation in advance during holidays.
  • Respect local customs, traditions and religious places.
  • Avoid littering and help keep tourist destinations clean.
