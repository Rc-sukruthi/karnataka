Discover the culture, history and natural beauty of southwestern India.

Introduction

Karnataka is a beautiful state located in the southwestern part of India. Its capital city is Bengaluru, which is popularly known as the Silicon Valley of India because of its growing information technology industry. Karnataka is known for its rich history, diverse culture, ancient temples, beautiful architecture, wildlife, waterfalls and scenic landscapes. The state was formed on 1 November 1956 and was originally known as Mysore State. It was renamed Karnataka on 1 November 1973. Kannada is the official language of Karnataka. The state celebrates several festivals, including Mysuru Dasara, Ugadi, Makara Sankranti and Karnataka Rajyotsava. Karnataka is also famous for its contributions to Indian literature, music, art, science and technology.

Quick Facts

  • Capital: Bengaluru
  • Official Language: Kannada
  • Population: Approximately 68 million (as of 2021)
  • Area: 191,791 square kilometers
  • Formation Day: 1 November 1956
  • Renamed: 1 November 1973
  • Region: Southwestern India
  • Major Rivers: Kaveri, Krishna, Tungabhadra and Sharavathi
  • Famous for: Mysuru Dasara, Hampi, Coorg, Jog Falls, and more.
